Welcome to Regy&Henry Foundation

A Unified Front Against Poverty, Exploitation, and Injustice.

RHAGF is a registered CAC/IT: 94632 non-governmental, non-profit and voluntary community-based organization established with a mission to transform and improve the quality of life among slum dwellers who face different challenges ranging from extreme poverty, poor health, human trafficking recruitment, drug abuse and peddling, sexual abuse, sexual exploitation and sex trafficking, illegal migration, out-of-school children, girl child education etc. hence our focus on these unskilled population such as the youth, vulnerable women and children thereby leveraging on Sustainable Development Goals (SDG’s)

No poverty | Zero Hunger | Good Health | Education | Gender Equality | Economic Growth| Partnership
